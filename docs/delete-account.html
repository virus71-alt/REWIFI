REWIFI Data Deletion

Last updated: August 29, 2026

REWIFI is operated by Doya Labs.

REWIFI does not create a separate REWIFI or Doya Labs user account. Google Sign-In is optional and is used only to enable Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

1. Delete Data Stored on Your Device

Wi-Fi network information saved in REWIFI is primarily stored locally on your device.

You can remove individual saved Wi-Fi networks directly inside REWIFI.

To remove all locally stored REWIFI data, you may also use Android's application settings:

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Open Apps.
  3. Select REWIFI.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Select Clear storage or Clear data.

Uninstalling REWIFI will also remove normal application data stored locally by the App, subject to Android's normal platform behavior.

2. Disconnect Google Drive

If you connected a Google account for Google Drive backup, you can disconnect it from REWIFI using the Google Drive connection controls available in the App.

Disconnecting your Google account removes REWIFI's active connection to that account on the device.

REWIFI does not receive or store your Google account password.

3. Delete REWIFI Backups from Google Drive

Disconnecting Google Drive does not automatically delete backup files that already exist in your Google Drive.

To permanently remove an existing REWIFI backup, open Google Drive using the Google account that was connected to REWIFI and delete the applicable REWIFI backup file.

Depending on your Google Drive interface, you may also need to empty the Google Drive trash to permanently remove a deleted file.

4. Revoke Google Authorization

You can also manage or revoke REWIFI's access to your Google account from your Google Account security settings.

Google provides account connection controls at:

https://myaccount.google.com/connections

Removing REWIFI's Google authorization prevents the App from continuing to use that authorization unless you connect again.

5. Exported or Shared Data

Files, QR codes, NFC tags, backups, or other information that you previously exported or shared outside REWIFI are not automatically deleted when REWIFI local data is cleared.

Those copies must be deleted from the application, device, storage provider, recipient, NFC tag, or other location where they were previously saved or shared.

6. What REWIFI Does Not Maintain

REWIFI does not operate a separate developer-controlled user account database for Google Sign-In.

Therefore, there is no separate REWIFI account record that users need to ask Doya Labs to delete.

Google account information and Google Drive data remain subject to your Google account and Google's applicable policies.
