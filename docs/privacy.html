Privacy Policy for REWIFI

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Doya Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the REWIFI mobile application ("REWIFI", the "App").

REWIFI is a Wi-Fi network utility designed to help users save, organize, protect, back up, restore, scan, and share Wi-Fi network information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information REWIFI processes, how that information is used and stored, when information may be transmitted to third-party services, and the choices available to users.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you choose to use, REWIFI may process information that you provide directly to the App, including:

  • Wi-Fi network names (SSIDs)
  • Wi-Fi passwords or security keys
  • Wi-Fi security types
  • Notes associated with saved Wi-Fi networks
  • Information contained in Wi-Fi QR codes that you scan
  • Information that you import into the App

This information is used to provide REWIFI's Wi-Fi management, QR sharing, backup, restore, NFC, and related functionality.

2. Local Storage

REWIFI is designed to keep your saved Wi-Fi information primarily on your device.

Saved network credentials and related vault information are stored using encrypted storage mechanisms. Cryptographic key material may be protected using Android security features including Android Keystore.

REWIFI may also store local application settings such as onboarding state, backup configuration, app-lock settings, synchronization information, and other preferences required for normal functionality.

3. Sensitive Wi-Fi Credentials

Wi-Fi passwords and other network credentials can be sensitive information. REWIFI processes this information only to provide features requested by the user, such as saving, displaying, sharing, restoring, or connecting to a network.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have permission to save or share Wi-Fi credentials belonging to another person, business, or organization.

4. Google Sign-In

REWIFI allows you to connect a Google account when you choose to use Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

When you sign in with Google, REWIFI may receive limited Google account information necessary to identify the connected account, such as:

  • Your Google account email address
  • A Google account identifier
  • Authentication and authorization information required for Google Drive access

REWIFI does not receive or store your Google account password. Your Google credentials are handled by Google.

5. Google Drive Backup and Restore

If you choose to connect Google Drive, REWIFI can create and restore encrypted backup data using your Google Drive account.

REWIFI requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This is intended to allow the App to access files that it creates or that you explicitly use with the App, rather than unrestricted access to all files in your Google Drive.

When this feature is used, encrypted backup data and related file metadata may be transmitted between your device and Google Drive.

Google Drive backup is optional. REWIFI's core local functionality can be used without connecting a Google account.

6. Backup Encryption

REWIFI is designed to protect backup data using encryption before or during storage according to the App's backup implementation.

Users should still protect access to their device, Google account, and exported backup files.

7. QR Code Scanning and Sharing

REWIFI can process Wi-Fi QR codes.

When you scan a Wi-Fi QR code, the App may read network information contained inside that code, including:

  • Network name
  • Password
  • Security type

When you generate or share a QR code, the information encoded in that QR code may be visible to anyone who receives or scans it.

8. Camera Access

REWIFI may request access to your device camera when you use QR scanning features.

Camera access is used for scanning supported QR codes. REWIFI does not use the camera to continuously record users.

9. NFC

REWIFI may provide NFC functionality that allows supported Wi-Fi information to be written to or processed from compatible NFC tags.

Information written to an NFC tag may be readable by other compatible devices. Users should avoid writing sensitive credentials to NFC tags that may be accessible to untrusted people.

10. Biometric Authentication

REWIFI may provide an optional biometric app-lock feature.

Biometric authentication is handled by Android's biometric security system. REWIFI does not receive or store your fingerprint, face scan, or raw biometric data.

The App receives only the authentication result provided by the Android operating system.

11. Wi-Fi Connection Features

REWIFI may use Android Wi-Fi or network connection APIs to help you connect to a Wi-Fi network that you select or scan.

The exact behavior may depend on your Android version and may require an Android system confirmation before connecting.

12. Files, Import, Export, and Sharing

REWIFI may allow you to export, import, save, or share backup files.

When you choose to share a file, the Android system share interface may allow you to send that file to another application or service.

After data is shared outside REWIFI, the selected recipient or external application controls its copy of that data according to its own privacy policy and terms.

13. Information REWIFI Does Not Intentionally Collect

REWIFI does not intentionally collect:

  • Credit or debit card information
  • Bank account credentials
  • Payment authentication information
  • Health or fitness information
  • SMS or MMS message contents
  • Email message contents
  • Your device contacts database
  • Microphone recordings
  • Videos
  • Web browsing history
  • Installed application lists for advertising purposes
  • Advertising ID for advertising purposes

14. Advertising

REWIFI does not contain third-party advertising and does not use an advertising SDK such as Google AdMob.

REWIFI does not use the Android Advertising ID for advertising purposes.

15. Analytics and Tracking

REWIFI does not use a general behavioral advertising or analytics platform for tracking users across applications or websites.

Google services used for Google Sign-In and Google Drive may process limited technical, security, operational, diagnostic, or service information required to provide those services.

16. Third-Party Services

REWIFI relies on certain Google services for optional features, including:

  • Google Sign-In / Google Play services
  • Google Drive API

These services may process information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

17. Internet Access

REWIFI may access the internet when using optional online features including Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup or restore.

Core local Wi-Fi vault functionality does not require a separate developer-controlled cloud backend.

18. Data Security

Doya Labs uses technical measures intended to protect information processed by REWIFI. Depending on the feature and device, these measures may include:

  • Encrypted local storage for sensitive application data
  • Android Keystore protection for cryptographic key material
  • Encrypted backup files
  • Optional biometric protection
  • HTTPS connections to Google services
  • Restricted Android file-sharing mechanisms

However, no electronic storage, encryption system, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

19. Data Retention

Information stored locally in REWIFI remains on your device until you delete the relevant records, clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove that information.

Backup files stored in Google Drive remain in your Google account until they are removed through REWIFI, Google Drive, or another applicable Google interface.

Files that you export or share may remain with the recipient or external application after they leave REWIFI.

20. Google Account and Drive Disconnection

REWIFI does not create a separate Doya Labs or REWIFI user account. Google Sign-In is used only for optional Google Drive-related functionality.

You may disconnect your Google account from REWIFI using the available disconnect functionality.

Disconnecting Google Sign-In does not necessarily delete backup files already stored in your Google Drive. Those files can be managed or deleted through your Google Drive account.

21. Data Deletion

Users can delete locally saved Wi-Fi information directly inside REWIFI.

Users can also clear REWIFI app data through Android system settings or uninstall the App to remove normal local application data.

Google Drive backup files can be deleted by the user from their own Google Drive account.

More information about deleting REWIFI data is available here:

https://virus71-alt.github.io/REWIFI/delete-account.html

22. Children's Privacy

REWIFI is a utility application intended for general users who need to manage Wi-Fi network information. It is not designed specifically for children.

Doya Labs does not knowingly design REWIFI to solicit personal information from children.

23. International Processing

Google services used by REWIFI may process information on servers located in countries other than your own.

Such processing is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies, terms, and legal obligations.

24. Your Choices

Depending on the feature, you may choose to:

  • Use REWIFI without connecting Google Drive
  • Connect or disconnect your Google account
  • Use or avoid Google Drive backup and restore
  • Enable or disable biometric app protection
  • Choose whether to scan QR codes
  • Choose whether to use NFC features
  • Edit or delete saved Wi-Fi information
  • Export or share backup information
  • Delete REWIFI backup files from your Google Drive

25. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Doya Labs may update this Privacy Policy when REWIFI's features, services, security practices, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be updated.
